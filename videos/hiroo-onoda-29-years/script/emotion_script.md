# Video Script: Hiroo Onoda — The Soldier Who Fought for 29 Years

## Overview
- **Topic:** Hiroo Onoda's 29-year holdout in the Philippine jungle
- **Format:** 9:16 Vertical Short
- **Tone:** Casual YouTube storyteller (Achird voice)

## Narration
In 1974, a man walked out of the jungle after being missing for 29 years. His name was Hiroo Onoda. In 1944, Japan sent him to a Philippine island with one mission: never surrender. Then World War II ended. Japan lost. But Onoda never believed it. He thought the surrender announcements were enemy propaganda. For decades, he and a few fellow soldiers hid in the dense jungle, surviving on bananas, coconuts, and stolen rice. Leaflets were dropped from airplanes. His family even sent letters and recordings, but he refused to believe any of them. One by one, his companions died or surrendered, until Onoda was completely alone, still fighting his private war. Until 1974, when a young Japanese traveler tracked him down deep in the jungle. Onoda demanded proof that the war was over. Japan flew his former commanding officer directly to the island, who gave him a direct order: You are relieved of your duties. Only then did Onoda lay down his rifle and sword. After 29 years, he finally came home. The war had ended before he even knew it.
