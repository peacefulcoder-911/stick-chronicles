# Video Script: Nalanda — The University That Attracted the World

## Overview
- **Topic:** Nalanda University
- **Format:** 9:16 Vertical Short
- **Tone:** Casual YouTube storyteller (Achird voice)

## Narration
More than a thousand years ago, students traveled across Asia to study at a university in India. It was called Nalanda. Located in present-day Bihar, it became one of the most important centers of Buddhist learning and scholarship. Students and teachers studied philosophy, logic, medicine, grammar, and other subjects. Chinese travelers, including Xuanzang, came to Nalanda and later described their experiences. Imagine a place where scholars from different regions gathered to debate ideas and study ancient texts. But Nalanda's history also includes destruction. Around the late 12th century, the institution was attacked during military campaigns in the region. Its buildings and collections suffered devastating damage. Today, its ruins remain in Bihar. And they tell us something extraordinary… India once had a university that attracted scholars from across the world, centuries before modern universities existed.
