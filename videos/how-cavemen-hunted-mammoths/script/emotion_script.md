## Narration
Think hunting a woolly mammoth was easy? These beasts were four meters tall with razor sharp tusks! A direct spear throw? That would just tickle it and make it furious. So cavemen spent days digging massive camouflage pit traps in the freezing permafrost. Then came the craziest job in human history: the human bait. Charging through the snow with a seven-ton monster right on his tail! At the last second, our boy leaps across the hidden pit. Crash! The mammoth plunges right in. The tribe swarms from the ridges, securing food for three whole months. Teamwork and big holes made us kings of the Ice Age.
