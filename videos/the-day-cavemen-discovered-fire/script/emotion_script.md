# Video Script: The Day Cavemen Discovered Fire

## Overview
- **Topic:** The accidental discovery and weaponization of fire
- **Format:** 9:16 Vertical Short
- **Pacing:** Fast, energetic, humorous
- **Tone:** Casual YouTube storyteller (Achird voice)

## Narration
50,000 years ago, being human was a 24/7 battle against freezing into an ice pop. Until nature decided to drop a lightning bolt right into a dry dead tree! Did our boy run away? Nope! He grabbed the burning branch and sprinted like an adrenaline-fueled maniac. And just like that, the food chain was flipped. Animals used to hunt us... now they check under the bed for us. Welcome to the dawn of humanity.
