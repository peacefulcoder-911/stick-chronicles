# Video Script: Why Do We Pay Taxes?

## Overview
- **Topic:** Why Do We Pay Taxes?
- **Format:** 9:16 Vertical Short
- **Tone:** Casual YouTube storyteller (Achird voice)

## Narration
Imagine earning 100 rupees. You want to keep all of it. But the government takes a portion. Why? Because the roads you drive on, the public schools children attend, and the hospitals many people depend on cost money. Taxes are how the government collects that money. Here's how it works. You earn money. You buy things. You own property. Depending on what you do, you may pay different taxes. For example, when you buy a 1,000 rupee product with 18% GST, the total becomes 1,180. That extra 180 is tax collected through the seller and paid to the government. But here's something interesting: not everyone pays the same amount. Income tax usually depends on how much you earn and the tax rules that apply to you. And businesses may pay taxes on their profits. So taxes aren't just money disappearing from your pocket. They're the system governments use to fund public services and infrastructure. The real question is: How well does the government use the money it collects?
